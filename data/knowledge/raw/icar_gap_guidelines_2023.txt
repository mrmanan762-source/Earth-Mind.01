1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE
Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) encompass environmental, economic, and social sustainability for on-farm processes resulting in safe and quality food and non-food agricultural products. In Indian farming systems, GAP aims at enhancing resource-use efficiency, reducing production costs, and preserving soil and water health.

2. CORE FARM MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES
- Land Selection & Site History: Select agricultural sites free from industrial heavy metal contamination and flood risks. Evaluate historic pesticide application records.
- Tillage & Soil Preparation: Adopt conservation tillage or minimum tillage practices wherever feasible to minimize soil compaction and prevent erosion of topsoil organic matter.
- Crop Rotation & Diversification: Rotate cereal crops with leguminous nitrogen-fixing crops (e.g., chickpea, pigeonpea, mungbean) every 2 to 3 seasons to break pest/weed lifecycles and replenish soil organic nitrogen.

3. SEED SELECTION AND SEED TREATMENT
- Certified Seed Usage: Farmers should utilize high-yielding, certified seed varieties registered with the State Seed Certification Agency or National Seeds Corporation (NSC).
- Seed Treatment Protocols: Treat seeds prior to sowing with bio-agents such as Trichoderma viride (4-5 g/kg seed) or Pseudomonas fluorescens (10 g/kg seed), or recommended chemical fungicides (e.g., Carbendazim 2 g/kg seed) to mitigate seed-borne pathogens.

4. INTEGRATED NUTRIENT AND PEST MANAGEMENT
- Nutrient Optimization: Soil test-based nutrient application must be prioritized. Integrate organic manures (FYM 10-12 tonnes/ha or vermicompost 2.5-3 tonnes/ha) with chemical fertilizers.
- Plant Protection Safety: Observe pre-harvest intervals (PHI) following pesticide applications. Only apply CPCB/CIBRC approved agrochemicals at recommended dosages using calibrated spraying equipment and protective gear.

5. HARVEST AND POST-HARVEST HYGIENE
- Harvest Timing: Harvest crops at physiological maturity when moisture content is optimal (e.g., 20-22% for cereals) to minimize shattering losses and fungal contamination.
- Drying & Storage: Sun-dry harvested grains down to safe storage moisture (12% for rice and wheat, 8-9% for oilseeds) prior to bag storage or metal bin storage. Keep storage structures clean, moisture-proof, and rodent-protected.
