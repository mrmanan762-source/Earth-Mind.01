1. CLIMATIC AND SOIL REQUIREMENTS
Soybean (Glycine max L.) is a warm-season kharif oilseed-cum-pulse; optimum temperature
25-30 degrees C. It requires 450-700 mm rainfall. Well-drained loam to clay loam with
pH 6.0-7.5 is ideal; it is sensitive to waterlogging and salinity.

2. GROWTH STAGES
Germination, vegetative growth, flowering, pod formation and pod filling. Flowering and
pod filling are the most moisture-sensitive stages.

3. SEED RATE, SPACING AND SOWING
Seed rate 60-75 kg/ha; spacing 45 cm x 5 cm (broadcast) or 30-45 cm x 10 cm (line
sowing). Sow with onset of monsoon in June-July. Inoculate seed with Rhizobium and
Bradyrhizobium japonicum; treat with Trichoderma against soil-borne diseases.

4. NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT (general principles)
Apply FYM 5-10 t/ha. General recommendation 30:60:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O per ha. Apply
20-30 kg sulphur per ha, important for oil and protein. Rhizobium inoculation improves
nitrogen fixation.

5. WATER REQUIREMENT
Requires 450-700 mm water. Largely rainfed; provide protective irrigation at flowering
and pod filling where available. Avoid waterlogging.

6. COMMON PESTS
Girdle beetle (Obereopsis brevis), stem fly, tobacco caterpillar (Spodoptera litura),
semilooper, green semilooper, whitefly, aphids.

7. COMMON DISEASES AND SYMPTOMS
Yellow mosaic virus (whitefly-borne): yellow mottling. Rhizoctonia aerial blight: web-like
mycelium on leaves. Charcoal rot: grey-black discolouration of stems. Bacterial pustule
and soybean mosaic virus.

8. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT
Use resistant varieties and treated seed; rotate with cereals; manage whitefly to reduce
yellow mosaic virus; use pheromone traps and light traps for tobacco caterpillar; follow
economic threshold levels; remove and destroy infected plants.

9. HARVESTING AND POST-HARVEST
Harvest when pods turn yellow-brown and seeds rattle; avoid shattering. Thresh and
store seed at 10-12 percent moisture.

10. CROP CALENDAR (indicative)
Kharif: sow June-July, harvest September-October.
