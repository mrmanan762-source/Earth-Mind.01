1. CLIMATIC AND SOIL REQUIREMENTS
Onion (Allium cepa L.) is a cool-season crop during vegetative growth and warm, dry weather
during bulb maturity. Optimum temperature 13-24 degrees C. Well-drained sandy loam with
pH 6.0-7.5 and good organic matter is ideal. Avoid heavy soils and waterlogging.

2. GROWTH STAGES
Nursery establishment, transplanting, vegetative growth, bulb initiation, bulb development,
maturity and curing.

3. NURSERY, SPACING AND TRANSPLANTING
Raise nursery on raised beds; seed rate 8-10 kg/ha. Transplant 6-8 week old seedlings at
15 cm x 10 cm spacing on flat beds or ridges. Kharif crop: May-June nursery, July
transplant. Rabi crop: October-November transplant. Late kharif: September transplant.

4. NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT (general principles)
Apply FYM 20-25 t/ha. General recommendation 100:50:50 kg N:P2O5:K2O per ha; apply half N
and full P, K as basal, remaining N in two splits. Sulphur improves pungency and yield;
include 20-30 kg sulphur per ha where deficient.

5. WATER REQUIREMENT
Requires 450-550 mm water. Irrigate lightly and frequently; stop irrigation 15-20 days
before harvest to allow bulb maturity and curing.

6. COMMON PESTS
Thrips (Thrips tabaci) causing silvery streaks and leaf curling; onion maggot; aphids;
mites.

7. COMMON DISEASES AND SYMPTOMS
Purple blotch (Alternaria porri): purple-centred lesions on leaves. Stemphylium blight:
leaf tip dieback. Downy mildew: pale elongated patches with fungal growth. Basal rot,
neck rot during storage.

8. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT
Use healthy seed and crop rotation with non-alliums; manage thrips with blue sticky traps
and recommended insecticides at threshold; avoid excess nitrogen; remove and destroy
infected plant debris; ensure proper curing to reduce storage rots.

9. HARVESTING AND POST-HARVEST
Harvest when 50 percent of tops fall over and neck dries. Cure in shade 10-15 days. Remove
tops, grade and store in ventilated, dry structures. Well-cured bulbs store 4-6 months.

10. CROP CALENDAR (indicative)
Kharif: transplant July, harvest October-November. Rabi: transplant November-December,
harvest April-May. Late kharif: transplant September, harvest December-January.
