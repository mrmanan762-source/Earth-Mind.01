1. CROP PHYSIOLOGY, TEMPERATURE SENSITIVITY & GROWTH HABITS
Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum L.) is a day-neutral, warm-season solanaceous vegetable crop grown across tropical and subtropical regions.
- Thermal Ranges & Sensitivity: Optimal growth occurs between 21°C and 24°C. Seed germination requires 20°C–25°C. Lycopene synthesis peaks between 20°C and 24°C, drops sharply above 27°C, and stops completely above 30°C. Daytime temperatures >32°C or night temperatures >21°C induce flower drop and poor fruit set due to pollen sterility and style elongation.
- Growth Habits & Staking: Classified into determinate (bushy, self-topping, uniform maturity ideal for processing/open-fields) and indeterminate (continuous vine growth requiring vertical staking/trellising and pruning, high yield over extended period).

2. VARIETIES, HYBRIDS & DISEASE RESISTANCE
- Multiple Disease Resistant Hybrids (ICAR-IIHR):
  * Arka Rakshak (F1): Triple disease resistant to Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV), Bacterial Wilt (BW), and Early Blight (EB). Yields 75–80 t/ha with firm 90–100g fruits.
  * Arka Samrat (F1): Triple disease resistant (ToLCV, BW, EB), high yield potential (80–85 t/ha), excellent shelf life.
  * Arka Abhed (F1): Quadruple disease resistant to ToLCV, BW, EB, and Late Blight (LB). Yields 80–90 t/ha under epiphytotic field conditions.
- Open-Pollinated & Regional Varieties: Pusa Ruby, Pusa Rohini, Arka Vikas, Arka Alok (BW resistant), Punjab Sartaj (PAU, low-tunnel/open-field), Pusa Red Cherry Tomato-3 (protected, 10.1 °Brix).

3. NURSERY, LAND PREPARATION & TRANSPLANTING
- Seed Rate & Nursery Management: Requires 100–125 g/ha for hybrids and 200–250 g/ha for open-pollinated varieties. Raise seedlings in 98-hole pro-trays using sterilized cocopeat or raised nursery beds (1 m width, 15 cm height). Treat seed with Trichoderma viride @ 4 g/kg or Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 10 g/kg seed. Cover beds with 40-mesh insect-proof netting for 25–30 days to prevent early whitefly vector infestation.
- Land Preparation & Spacing: Prepare broad beds of 1.2 m top width with 30 cm furrows. Layout drip laterals and cover with 25-micron silver-on-black polyethylene mulch. Transplant 25–30 day old seedlings (12–15 cm height) at 60 cm x 45 cm for determinate types or 90 cm x 45 cm for indeterminate hybrids (~22,000–24,000 plants/ha).

4. NUTRIENT & DRIP FERTIGATION MANAGEMENT
- Fertilizer Requirements: Baseline N:P2O5:K2O dose is 150:100:100 kg/ha for open-pollinated varieties and 200:250:250 kg/ha for commercial drip-irrigated hybrids, plus 25 t/ha FYM and biofertilizers (Azospirillum & PSB @ 2 kg/ha each).
- Drip Fertigation Schedule: Apply 1/5th Nitrogen and 100% Phosphorus as basal. Apply remaining N and Potassium through drip weekly from 2 to 12 weeks post-transplanting.
- Foliar Spray & Secondary Nutrients: Apply Calcium Nitrate @ 0.5% (5 g/L) at 30 and 45 DAT to prevent Blossom-End Rot (BER). Spray Borax @ 0.2% (2 g/L) at flowering to prevent fruit cracking and hollow cavities.

5. INTEGRATED PEST & DISEASE MANAGEMENT (IPDM)
- Diagnostic Uncertainty Directive: Diagnostic symptoms on leaves or fruits should be confirmed via laboratory ELISA/PCR testing or multi-symptom field evaluations before applying restricted systemic pesticides.
- Major Insect Pests:
  * Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera): Larva bores round holes into fruits. ETL: 1 egg/plant or 1 damaged fruit/plant. Plant marigold (Tagetes erecta) as trap crop (1 row marigold per 16 rows tomato). Install pheromone traps @ 12/ha. Spray HaNPV @ 250 LE/ha or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L.
  * Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci): Vector for ToLCV. Install yellow sticky traps @ 25/ha. Spray Neem oil 3% (30 ml/L) or Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 ml/L at early vegetative stage.
- Major Diseases:
  * Early Blight (Alternaria solani): Concentric target-board rings on older leaves. Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1.0 ml/L.
  * Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans): Water-soaked lesions with white cottony growth under high humidity (>90% RH) and cool temperatures (15–20°C). Spray Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
  * Bacterial Wilt (Ralstonia solanacearum): Rapid green wilting without yellowing; vascular browning and bacterial ooze in clear water. Use resistant hybrids (Arka Rakshak, Arka Abhed) and soil drenching with Streptocycline @ 200 ppm (0.2 g/L) + Copper Oxychloride @ 3.0 g/L.

6. HARVESTING, GRADING & POST-HARVEST MANAGEMENT
- Harvest Stages: Harvest at 'Breaker stage' (10–20% color change at blossom end) for distant marketing, or 'Turning/Pink stage' for local markets.
- Post-Harvest Handling & Storage: Pre-cool harvested tomatoes to 12–13°C. Store at 12–15°C with 85–90% relative humidity. Never store below 10°C to prevent chilling injury (pitting, water-soaking, failure to ripen). Pack in ventilated CFB corrugated boxes or plastic crates (10–15 kg capacity).
