1. CLIMATIC AND SOIL REQUIREMENTS
Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum L.) grows best at 20-27 degrees C. Temperatures above 35
degrees C cause flower drop; frost damages the crop. Well-drained sandy loam to loam with
pH 6.0-7.0 and good organic matter is ideal.

2. GROWTH STAGES
Nursery, transplanting, vegetative growth, flowering, fruit set, fruit development and
ripening. Flowering and fruit set are the most sensitive stages.

3. NURSERY, SPACING AND TRANSPLANTING
Raise nursery in raised beds; seed rate 400-500 g/ha. Transplant 25-30 day old seedlings
at 60 cm x 45 cm on raised beds or 75 cm x 60 cm in hybrids. Treat nursery soil against
damping off.

4. NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT (general principles)
Apply FYM 20-25 t/ha. General recommendation 120:60:60 kg N:P2O5:K2O per ha; apply half N
and full P, K as basal and remaining N in two splits at flowering and fruit set. Support
with calcium and boron to reduce fruit cracking and blossom-end rot.

5. WATER REQUIREMENT
Requires 400-600 mm water. Irrigate at 5-7 day intervals; drip irrigation with mulching is
recommended to conserve water and reduce fruit rot.

6. COMMON PESTS
Fruit borer (Helicoverpa armigera), whitefly (Bemisia tabaci, a virus vector), leaf miner,
aphids, thrips, mites.

7. COMMON DISEASES AND SYMPTOMS
Early blight (Alternaria solani): concentric ring lesions. Late blight: water-soaked
lesions with white mould. Leaf curl virus (transmitted by whitefly): upward curling and
stunting. Bacterial wilt: sudden wilting. Damping off in nursery.

8. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT
Use resistant/tolerant varieties; use healthy nursery; install yellow sticky traps for
whitefly; use pheromone traps for fruit borer; release Trichogramma; rotate with
non-solanaceous crops; remove infected plants (leaf curl, bacterial wilt); follow economic
threshold levels.

9. HARVESTING AND POST-HARVEST
Harvest at breaker to pink stage for distant markets and red-ripe for local markets. Handle
gently; grade and pack in ventilated crates. Cool storage extends shelf life.

10. CROP CALENDAR (indicative)
Kharif: transplant June-July, harvest September-October. Rabi: transplant October-November,
harvest February-March. Summer: transplant January-February, harvest May-June.
