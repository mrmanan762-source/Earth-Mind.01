1. TAXONOMIC CLASSIFICATION, BOTANY & DESI VS KABULI TYPES
Chickpea (Cicer arietinum L., 2n=16) is the leading pulse crop in India, accounting for 49% of national pulse output.
- Desi Type (Cicer arietinum var. arietinum): Characterized by small, angular, dark-colored seeds (yellowish-brown to black), anthocyanin-pigmented purple flowers/stems, high seed coat fiber and tannins, and 100-seed weight of 17–23 g. Represents 80–85% of total Indian production.
- Kabuli Type (Cicer arietinum var. marmoratum): Characterized by large, ram-head shaped, beige/white seeds, white flowers, unpigmented green plants, thin seed coat, low fiber, and 100-seed weight of 26–45 g.
- Nutritional Profile: Seeds contain 18.0–22.0% protein, 60–65% carbohydrates, 4.5–6.0% lipids, and rich micronutrients (iron, zinc, folate).

2. CLIMATE, SOIL & REGIONAL VARIETIES
- Climate & Thermal Sensitivity: Cool-season legume requiring 15°C–25°C optimum growth temperature. Extremely sensitive to frost at flowering/pod set and high temperature/heat stress (>30°C) during flowering causing pollen sterility and pod abortion. Sowing window: October to November (NWPZ, NEPZ, Central Zone, Southern Zone).
- Soil & Moisture: Thrives in well-drained loams, clay loams, and deep black cotton soils (pH 6.0–8.0). Highly sensitive to soil salinity and waterlogging.
- Improved Varieties (ICAR-IIPR / AICRP):
  * DCP 92-3 (Central Zone / NWPZ): Desi, yield 20–25 q/ha, wilt resistant.
  * JAKI 9218 (Central & Southern Zones): Desi, early maturing (105–110 days), wilt resistant, yield 18–22 q/ha.
  * JG 11 (Central & Southern India): Desi, short duration (95–100 days), wilt resistant, ideal for drought-prone rainfed areas.
  * IPC 2007-28 / Atal (Northern Plains): Desi, yield 22–26 q/ha, wilt & Ascochyta resistant.
  * IPC 2010-134 / Shiva (NWPZ/NEPZ): Desi, yield 20–24 q/ha.
  * IPC 2005-62 (High Protein): Desi, >24% protein content.
  * KMN 1 / IPCK 2004-29 (Kabuli): Large seeded Kabuli, 100-seed weight >38 g, yield 18–22 q/ha.

3. SOWING, SEED TREATMENT & DENSITY MANAGEMENT
- Seed Rate & Spacing:
  * Desi Cultivars: 60–75 kg/ha at 30 cm x 10 cm spacing.
  * Kabuli Cultivars: 90–120 kg/ha at 45 cm x 10 cm spacing.
  * Late-Sown Crops: 80–90 kg/ha at 30 cm x 8–10 cm. Sowing depth: 7–10 cm under rainfed conditions to reach subsoil moisture; 5–7 cm under irrigated regimes.
- Seed Treatment & Bio-Inoculation: Seed dressing with Trichoderma viride @ 4 g/kg seed or Carbendazim (1 g) + Thiram (2 g/kg seed) against wilt and root rot. Bio-inoculate with Mesorhizobium ciceri and Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 20 g/kg seed. Apply hydrogel @ 2.5–5.0 kg/ha in dryland soils to enhance root-zone water retention.

4. NUTRIENT & IRRIGATION MANAGEMENT
- Basal Fertilizer Recommendation: Basal N:P2O5:K2O:S @ 20–30:40–60:20–30:20 kg/ha. Biological N fixation contributes 60–80 kg N/ha. Under rainfed moisture stress, apply foliar spray of 2% Urea or 2% DAP at pre-flowering and pod initiation stages.
- Irrigation Scheduling: Requires 1–2 light irrigations (2–3 cm depth). Critical stages: branching/pre-flowering (35–45 DAS) and pod formation (70–80 DAS). Avoid irrigation during peak flowering to prevent flower drop and excess vegetative growth.

5. INTEGRATED PEST, DISEASE & WEED MANAGEMENT
- Weed Control: Pre-emergence Pendimethalin 30% EC @ 1.0 kg a.i./ha (3.3 L/ha) within 3 days of sowing, or post-emergence Topramezone 33.6% SC @ 20.6 g a.i./ha or Imazethapyr 10% SL @ 50–75 g a.i./ha at 20–25 DAS.
- Major Diseases:
  * Fusarium Wilt (Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. ciceris): Soil-borne, vascular browning, yellowing, drooping. Deploy resistant varieties (DCP 92-3, JAKI 9218, JG 11, Atal, Shiva); treat seed with Trichoderma + Carbendazim; follow 3–4 year crop rotation.
  * Dry Root Rot (Rhizoctonia bataticola): Triggered by high temperature (>30°C) and moisture stress; roots turn brittle and straw-colored.
  * Ascochyta Blight (Ascochyta rabiei): Cool humid weather in NWPZ; brown circular spots on leaves/pods. Spray Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
- Major Insect Pests:
  * Gram Pod Borer (Helicoverpa armigera): Larva bores into pods and consumes seeds. ETL: 1 larva/meter row or 5–10% pod damage. Install pheromone traps @ 12/ha; bird perches @ 50/ha; spray HaNPV @ 250 LE/ha; chemical spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Emamectin benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Indoxacarb 14.5% SC @ 0.5 ml/L.

6. HARVESTING, POST-HARVEST & ECONOMICS
- Maturity & Threshing: Harvest when leaves dry and pods turn straw-yellow (seed moisture 15–18%). Dry threshed grain to 10–12% moisture.
- Dal Milling & Storage: Process using IIPR Dal Chakki or Mini Dal Mill. Store in airtight bins; protect against Pulse Beetle (Callosobruchus chinensis) using dried neem leaf powder @ 1–2% w/w or neem oil @ 10 ml/kg seed.
- Macro-Economics: Average yield 18.5–22.0 q/ha (potential 25 q/ha). Cultivation cost: ₹20,000–22,000/ha; Gross returns: ₹55,000–62,000/ha; Net profit: ₹35,000–42,000/ha; Benefit-Cost (B:C) ratio: 2.5–2.8.
