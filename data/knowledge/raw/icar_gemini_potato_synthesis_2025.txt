1. CROP OVERVIEW, HISTORICAL FOOTPRINT, AND ECONOMIC SIGNIFICANCE
- Taxonomic Classification: Solanum tuberosum L. Solanaceous tuberous perennial crop grown as an annual.
- National Footprint: India is the second-largest global producer (45.87-54.6 MT annually). Contributes 2.86-3.7% of total agricultural GDP while occupying only 1.32-2.8% of net cultivable land area.
- Per-Unit Value Contribution: Potato generates 3.7x more value per hectare than rice and 5.4x more than wheat.
- Direct Labor Employment: Generates ~293 million man-days of direct agricultural employment annually (145 man-days/ha requirement).
- Utilization Distribution: 68% fresh domestic consumption, 7.5% industrial processing, 8.5% seed tubers, 16% post-harvest handling and cold chain loss.

2. PHENOLOGICAL GROWTH STAGES AND TUBER DEVELOPMENT
- Five Distinct Development Phases:
  a. Sprout Development (Phase I): Emergence of sprouts from seed tuber eyes utilizing seed food reserves (0-15 DAP).
  b. Vegetative Growth (Phase II): Emergence of leaves, main stems, lateral branches, and roots (15-35 DAP).
  c. Tuber Initiation / Stolon Swelling (Phase III): Tips of stolons swell to form young tuber initials (30-45 DAP).
  d. Tuber Bulking (Phase IV): Rapid accumulation of water, dry matter, and starch in expanding tubers (45-75 DAP).
  e. Tuber Maturation (Phase V): Canopy senesces, tuber periderm (skin) thickens and thickens for storage (75-105+ DAP).

3. AGRO-ECOLOGICAL ZONATION AND GEOGRAPHIC DISTRIBUTION
- North Indian Indo-Gangetic Plains (86% of National Area): Sub-tropical winter (Rabi) crop (UP, West Bengal, Bihar, Punjab, MP).
- Peninsular Plateau (8% of Area): Kharif and Rabi production (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat).
- Northern & Southern Hills (6% of Area): Temperate summer crop (Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Nilgiris).

4. CLIMATE REQUIREMENTS AND THERMAL THRESHOLDS
- Temperature Sensitivity for Tuberization:
  - Optimum Tuberization Range: 18°C to 20°C daytime, 12°C to 14°C night temperature.
  - Critical Thermal Limit: Night temperatures > 20°C severely suppress tuberization by diverting photosynthates to vegetative shoot growth.
  - Frost Vulnerability: Foliage is killed by freezing temperatures (-1°C to -2°C).

5. SOIL REQUIREMENTS, DRAINAGE, PH, AND FIELD PREPARATION
- Soil Suitability: Deep, friable, well-drained sandy loams or silt loams rich in organic matter.
- pH Preference: Slightly acidic to neutral soils (pH 5.2 to 6.5). Avoid alkaline soils (pH > 7.5) which favor Streptomyces scabies (Common Scab).
- Field Preparation: Deep ploughing to 25-30 cm depth followed by 2-3 harrowings and planking to establish a pulverized, loose seedbed free of clods.

6. VARIETAL DYNAMICS AND CULTIVAR MATRIX
- Table Cultivars: Kufri Jyoti (medium duration, wide adaptability), Kufri Bahar (short/medium duration, white tubers), Kufri Pukhraj (early maturing 70-80 days, high yielding), Kufri Ratan (released 2025: red-skinned, 75-90 days, 18% dry matter, resistant to Late Blight).
- Processing Cultivars: Kufri Chipsona-1, Kufri Chipsona-3, Kufri Frysona (high dry matter >20%, low reducing sugars <0.1% to prevent browning).
- Heat Tolerant Varieties: Kufri Surya (suitable for early autumn planting in warm plains).

7. SEED TUBER SELECTION, SEED PLOT TECHNIQUE, AND AEROPONICS
- Seed Tuber Standards: Well-sprouted, disease-free seed tubers of 30-45g weight (35-45 mm diameter).
- Seed Rate: 2.5 to 3.0 tonnes of seed tubers per hectare (1.0-1.2 t/acre).
- Seed Treatment SOP: Dip cut or whole seed tubers in Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L water or Boric Acid 3% solution for 30 minutes to prevent tuber-borne black scurf and dry rot.
- Seed Plot Technique (ICAR-CPRI Innovation): Raising aphid-free seed crops during low-aphid window (Oct-Dec) in Northern plains using dehaulming before vector populations exceed ETL (20 aphids/100 leaves).

8. PLANTING SEASONS, SPACING, AND EARTHING-UP
- Planting Windows:
  - North Indian Plains (Main Rabi Crop): October 1 to October 25.
  - Plateau Kharif Crop: June to July.
  - Hill Summer Crop: March to April.
- Spacing Standard: 60 cm row-to-row x 20 cm tuber-to-tuber (55,555 plants/ha).
- Planting Depth: 5 to 7 cm deep on ridges.
- Earthing-Up SOP: Mandatory earthing-up at 25-30 DAP (when plants are 15-20 cm high) to form broad ridges, cover developing stolons, promote tuber bulking, and prevent tuber greening (solanine synthesis).

9. NUTRIENT AND FERTILIZER MANAGEMENT
- Baseline Recommended Dosage: 150-180 kg N : 80-100 kg P2O5 : 100-120 kg K2O per hectare + FYM @ 25 t/ha.
- Split Application Schedule:
  - Basal Application: 50% N + 100% P2O5 + 100% K2O at planting in furrows.
  - Top Dressing: 50% N at first earthing-up (25-30 DAP).
- Potash Requirement: Potato is a heavy feeder of Potassium; apply Muriate of Potash (MOP) or Sulfate of Potash (SOP) to enhance tuber starch accumulation and shelf life.
- Micronutrient SOP: Apply Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 25 kg/ha basal in deficient soils.

10. IRRIGATION SCHEDULING, WATER STRESS, AND DRAINAGE
- Critical Irrigation Stages:
  1. Sprout Emergence & Stolon Initiation (15-25 DAP).
  2. Tuber Initiation (30-40 DAP) - MOST CRITICAL.
  3. Tuber Bulking Stage (45-75 DAP).
- Irrigation Interval: Apply light irrigation every 7-10 days depending on soil texture.
- Water Termination: Stop irrigation 10-12 days before dehaulming/harvesting to allow skin hardening and reduce soft rot incidence during storage.

11. WEED MANAGEMENT AND HERBICIDE SCHEDULES
- Critical Weed Competition Window: First 30-45 DAP until crop canopy closes.
- Chemical Herbicide Schedules:
  - Pre-emergence: Apply Metribuzin 70% WP @ 0.7 kg a.i./ha (1.0 kg/ha product) at 3-5 days after planting in 500L water.
  - Early Post-emergence: Apply Paraquat dichloride 24% SL @ 0.5 kg a.i./ha when 5% potato sprouts have emerged.

12. INSECT PESTS AND VECTOR IPM
- Green Peach Aphid (Myzus persicae) & Potato Aphid:
  - Direct Damage & Virus Vector: Sucks sap and transmits Potato Virus Y (PVY) and Potato Leafroll Virus (PLRV).
  - Monitoring & Vector ETL: Deploy yellow sticky traps. ETL for seed crops is 20 aphids per 100 compound leaves.
  - Control SOP: Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 150 ml/ha or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 100 g/ha.
- Cutworms (Agrotis ipsilon): Cut young plants at ground level at night. Managed via Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2.5 L/ha soil drenching.

13. MAJOR DISEASES AND INTEGRATED CONTROL
- Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans) - Most Destructive Disease:
  - Diagnostic Symptoms: Water-soaked dark brown leaf lesions expanding rapidly with white cottony fungal growth on lower leaf surfaces under high humidity (>90%) and cool temps (10-20°C).
  - Prophylactic Spray: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L prior to disease onset.
  - Curative Spray upon Disease Detection: Spray Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L.
- Early Blight (Alternaria solani): Concentric target-board brown rings on older leaves. Controlled via Mancozeb or Chlorothalonil @ 2.0 g/L.
- Bacterial Wilt / Brown Rot (Ralstonia solanacearum): Sudden wilting of plants; vascular browning and bacterial ooze from cut tubers. Managed via crop rotation with maize/wheat and using certified disease-free seed tubers.

14. NUTRIENT DEFICIENCIES VS DISEASE DIAGNOSTICS
- Potassium Deficiency: Leaf margins turn dark green, cup downwards, develop brown marginal scorching and necrotic spots.
- Zinc Deficiency: Interveinal chlorosis and bronzing on young leaves.
- Contrast with Late Blight: Late blight presents rapidly spreading wet brown necrotic blights with white mildew underneath, not dry marginal leaf scorching.

15. WEATHER RISKS AND CLIMATE RESILIENCE
- Frost Risk Mitigation: Irrigate crop lightly on expected frost nights or create light smoke around fields to elevate canopy temperature by 1-2°C.

16. HARVEST MATURITY, DEHAULMING, CURING, AND STORAGE
- Dehaulming SOP for Seed/Storage Crop: Cut or spray Diquat/Paraquat to kill green vines 10-15 days before harvest. Dehaulming stops tuber growth, promotes periderm skin maturation, and prevents late blight spore wash to tubers.
- Curing SOP: Keep harvested tubers in dark, cool, ventilated sheds at 15-20°C and 85-90% relative humidity for 10-15 days to heal skin wounds/cuts.
- Cold Storage Conditions:
  - Seed Tubers: Store at 2°C to 4°C with 85-90% RH to maintain dormant, disease-free seed status.
  - Processing Tubers: Store at 8°C to 10°C treated with CIPC (Isopropyl N-3-chlorophenylcarbamate) sprout inhibitor to prevent accumulation of reducing sugars and cold-induced sweetening.

17. TUBER QUALITY PROBLEMS AND TROUBLESHOOTING
- Tuber Greening & Solanine Toxicity: Exposure of tubers to sunlight due to inadequate earthing-up generates toxic glycoalkaloid solanine (green tubers are unfit for consumption).
- Hollow Heart: Internal cavity forming in large tubers due to rapid irregular tuber bulking under excessive irrigation/nitrogen.

18. PRODUCTION ECONOMICS AND YIELD BASELINES
- Yield Baselines: Irrigated plains average 30-40 t/ha (Kufri Pukhraj / Kufri Ratan); processing cultivars yield 30-35 t/ha.
- Cost-Benefit Ratio: Potato achieves C:B ratios of 2.1 to 2.8 under disease-free high-input management.

19. FARMER DECISION GUIDANCE AND DIAGNOSTIC UNCERTAINTY
- Visual Diagnostic Uncertainty Note: Visual symptom identification for viral diseases (PVY vs PLRV) or bacterial wilt carries diagnostic uncertainty; confirm via ELISA testing or lab PCR before discarding entire seed lots.
