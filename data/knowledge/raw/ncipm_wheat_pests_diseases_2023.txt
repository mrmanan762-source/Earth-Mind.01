1. MAJOR WHEAT DISEASES & IDENTIFICATION
- Yellow (Stripe) Rust (Puccinia striiformis): Identified by bright yellow pustules arranged in linear stripes on leaves and leaf sheaths. Prevalent in cooler sub-mountainous and northwestern plains zone (NWPZ).
- Brown (Leaf) Rust (Puccinia triticina): Identified by small, round, orange-brown pustules scattered irregularly on the leaf blades.
- Karnal Bunt (Tilletia indica): Fungal spores attack individual grains during flowering, causing partial blackening and a foul fishy odor due to trimethylamine emission.
- Loose Smut (Ustilago nuda var. tritici): Entire spike converted into a black powdery mass of smudged teliospores.

2. MAJOR WHEAT PESTS & DAMAGE SYMPTOMS
- Termites (Odontotermes obesus): Attack crop at sowing/seedling and maturing stages, cutting roots below ground level causing deadhearts and drying of tillers.
- Wheat Aphid (Sitobion avenae / Rhopalosiphum maidis): Nymphs and adults suck sap from tender leaves and developing ears, causing yellowing and honey-dew accumulation leading to sooty mold.
- Armyworm (Mythimna separata): Caterpillars devour foliage during nighttime and cut off ears at maturity.

3. MANAGEMENT STRATEGIES
- Disease Management:
  * For Yellow/Brown Rust: Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 500 ml/ha or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 625 ml/ha in 500 liters of water upon first symptom detection.
  * For Karnal Bunt: Avoid excessive nitrogenous fertilisation and flood irrigation at flowering stage. Spray Tilt 25 EC @ 0.1% at boot stage.
  * For Loose Smut: Seed treatment with Carboxin 75% WP @ 2.5 g/kg seed or Vitavax Power @ 2.0 g/kg seed.
- Insect Pest Management:
  * For Termites: Treat seed with Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 4 ml/kg seed or Imidacloprid 600 FS @ 3 ml/kg seed prior to sowing.
  * For Aphids: Encourage natural predators (Coccinella septempunctata ladybird beetles). If aphid population exceeds Economic Threshold Level (ETL) of 10-15 aphids/earhead, spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 100 g/ha.
