1. CLIMATIC AND SOIL REQUIREMENTS
Potato (Solanum tuberosum L.) is a cool-season crop; optimum growth at 15-25 degrees C.
Tuberisation is best at 17-20 degrees C. Frost and high temperature (above 30 degrees C)
reduce yield. Well-drained sandy loam to loam soils rich in organic matter, pH 5.5-6.5,
are ideal.

2. GROWTH STAGES
Sprouting, vegetative growth, tuber initiation, tuber bulking, maturity. Tuber bulking is
the most critical stage for water and nutrients.

3. SEED TUBER, SPACING AND PLANTING
Use healthy, certified seed tubers 30-40 g. Seed rate about 2000-2500 kg/ha. Spacing
60 cm x 20 cm. Plant in October-November (plains) and March-April (hills). Cut surfaces
of cut seed should be treated with fungicide and cured before planting.

4. NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT (general principles)
Apply FYM 20-25 t/ha. General recommendation 150-180:80-100:100-120 kg N:P2O5:K2O per ha.
Apply half N and full P, K as basal; remaining N at earthing up. Avoid excess nitrogen
which delays tuberisation. Use soil test based recommendations where available.

5. WATER REQUIREMENT
Requires 500-600 mm water. Irrigate at 7-10 day intervals; maintain adequate soil moisture
during tuber bulking; stop irrigation 10-15 days before harvest to harden the skin.

6. COMMON PESTS
Aphids (also virus vectors), potato tuber moth (Phthorimaea operculella), cutworms, white
grubs, mites.

7. COMMON DISEASES AND SYMPTOMS
Late blight (Phytophthora infestans): water-soaked lesions on leaves turning brown with
white sporulation on the underside in humid cool weather. Early blight (Alternaria solani):
concentric ring spots. Common scab, black scurf, bacterial wilt (sudden wilting).

8. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT
Use disease-free seed; destroy culled tubers; rotate with cereals; dehaulm (cut foliage)
10-15 days before harvest to reduce tuber infection; use recommended fungicides
(mancozeb, metalaxyl-based) preventively during cool, humid weather.

9. HARVESTING AND POST-HARVEST
Harvest when haulms dry and skin is set. Cure tubers in shade. Store in cool, dark,
ventilated stores; cold storage at 2-4 degrees C for table potatoes.

10. CROP CALENDAR (indicative)
Plains (rabi): plant October-November, harvest January-March. Hills (summer): plant
March-April, harvest July-August.
