1. CLIMATIC AND SOIL REQUIREMENTS
Pigeonpea (Cajanus cajan L.) is a warm-season kharif pulse; optimum temperature 20-30
degrees C. It is drought tolerant and deep-rooted. Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam
with pH 6.0-7.5 is ideal; it is sensitive to waterlogging.

2. GROWTH STAGES
Germination, vegetative growth, branching, flowering, pod formation and pod filling.
Pigeonpea is a long-duration crop (150-270 days) with indeterminate growth habit.

3. SEED RATE, SPACING AND SOWING
Seed rate 12-15 kg/ha for long-duration and 20-25 kg/ha for short-duration types. Spacing
90-150 cm x 20 cm for long-duration; 45-60 cm x 15-20 cm for short-duration. Sow with
onset of monsoon in June-July. Pigeonpea is often intercropped with sorghum, soybean or
groundnut.

4. NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT (general principles)
Apply a starter dose of 25 kg N/ha; general recommendation 25:50:25 kg N:P2O5:K2O per
ha. Apply 20-30 kg sulphur per ha where deficient. Rhizobium inoculation improves
nitrogen fixation.

5. WATER REQUIREMENT
Requires 400-650 mm water. Largely rainfed; provide protective irrigation at flowering and
pod filling where available. Avoid waterlogging.

6. COMMON PESTS
Pod borer complex (Helicoverpa armigera, Maruca vitrata, Melanagromyza obtusa),
pod fly, pod bug, pod wasp, termites.

7. COMMON DISEASES AND SYMPTOMS
Wilt (Fusarium udum): gradual yellowing and wilting. Sterility mosaic disease (mite-borne):
yellowing and sterility. Phytophthora blight in waterlogged conditions. Alternaria blight.

8. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT
Use wilt-resistant and sterility-mosaic-resistant varieties; intercropping reduces pod borer
incidence; install pheromone traps and bird perches; use HaNPV and recommended
insecticides at threshold; rotate and remove infected plants.

9. HARVESTING AND POST-HARVEST
Harvest when 75-80 percent pods mature; avoid over-maturity to prevent shattering. Dry,
thresh and store at 9-12 percent moisture.

10. CROP CALENDAR (indicative)
Kharif: sow June-July, harvest December-January (short-duration) to February-March
(long-duration).
