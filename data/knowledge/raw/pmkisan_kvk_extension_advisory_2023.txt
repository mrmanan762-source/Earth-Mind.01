1. KVK MANDATE AND EXTENSION SERVICES
Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) function as agricultural technology dissemination centers established by ICAR at the district level across India. Key operational mandates include:
- On-Farm Testing (OFT): Assessing location-specific agricultural technologies under local micro-farming conditions.
- Frontline Demonstrations (FLDs): Demonstrating newly released high-yielding varieties, crop management technologies, and farm machinery directly on farmers' fields.
- Capacity Development & Training: Organizing vocational training courses for farmers, farm women, rural youth, and agricultural field officers.

2. PM-KISAN FARMER ADVISORY INTEGRATION
Under the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme, direct benefit transfers are complemented by seasonal agromet advisory messages delivered via mKisan portal and KVK network.
- Weather-Based Crop Advisories: Issue bi-weekly advisories based on India Meteorological Department (IMD) Agromet district forecasts covering rainfall, temperature, wind speed, and relative humidity.
- Climate Resilience Guidelines: Encourage adoption of climate-resilient crop varieties (e.g., drought-tolerant paddy, heat-tolerant wheat like HD 3385 / DBW 303).

3. AGRICULTURAL EXTENSION DISSEMINATION PROTOCOLS
- Village Agro-Advisory Committee: KVK Subject Matter Specialists (SMS) in Agronomy, Plant Protection, Soil Science, and Horticulture conduct monthly field visits to identify emerging pest outbreaks and field difficulties.
- Standardized Advisory Format: All agricultural advisories issued to farmers must include:
  1. Current crop growth stage.
  2. Detected pest/disease or weather advisory threshold.
  3. Actionable recommendation specifying chemical/biological agent, precise dose per acre/hectare, and safety precautions.
