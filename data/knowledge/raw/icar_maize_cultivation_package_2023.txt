1. CLIMATIC AND SOIL REQUIREMENTS
Maize (Zea mays L.) is a warm-season (kharif and rabi) cereal adapted to temperatures of
21-27 degrees C. It is sensitive to frost and waterlogging. Deep, well-drained loams with
pH 6.0-7.5 and good organic matter are ideal. Maize responds strongly to nitrogen.

2. GROWTH STAGES
Germination (0-7 d), seedling (7-20 d), vegetative/tasselling (20-55 d), silking and
pollination (55-65 d), grain filling (65-95 d), physiological maturity (95-110 d).
Critical moisture stages: tasselling, silking and grain filling.

3. SEED RATE, SPACING AND SOWING
Seed rate 20-25 kg/ha; spacing 60 cm x 20 cm for grain hybrids (75,000-83,000 plants/ha).
Sow kharif with onset of monsoon (June-July) and rabi in October-November. Treat seed with
Trichoderma viride (5 g/kg) or recommended fungicide against seed-borne pathogens.

4. NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT (general principles)
Apply farmyard manure 10 t/ha. General recommendation for irrigated maize is 120:60:40
kg N:P2O5:K2O per ha, with half N and full P and K as basal and remaining N in two splits
at knee-high and pre-tasselling. Zinc sulphate 25 kg/ha where deficiency is known.

5. WATER REQUIREMENT
Irrigated maize requires 500-600 mm water. Irrigate at critical stages (tasselling, silking,
grain filling). Avoid waterlogging; ensure drainage.

6. COMMON PESTS
Fall armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda), stem borer (Chilo partellus), shoot fly, aphids,
termites. Scout whorls for fall armyworm larvae and fresh frass.

7. COMMON DISEASES AND SYMPTOMS
Turcicum leaf blight (elongated grey-green lesions), maydis leaf blight (diamond-shaped
lesions), downy mildew (chlorotic streaks), banded leaf and sheath blight, smut (galls).

8. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT
Rotate with legumes; destroy crop residues; use pheromone traps for stem borer; encourage
natural enemies; use recommended bio-pesticides (Bacillus thuringiensis, NPV for armyworm)
before chemical control; follow economic threshold levels.

9. HARVESTING AND POST-HARVEST
Harvest at physiological maturity (black layer at kernel base), around 20-25 percent grain
moisture. Dry to 12-14 percent before storage. Store in clean, dry, aerated structures.

10. CROP CALENDAR (indicative)
Kharif: sow June-July, harvest September-October. Rabi: sow October-November, harvest
March-April. Timelines vary by region and variety duration.
