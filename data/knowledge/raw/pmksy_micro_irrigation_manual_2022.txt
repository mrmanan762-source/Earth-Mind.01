1. INTRODUCTION & WATER USE EFFICIENCY
Micro-irrigation techniques (Drip and Sprinkler Irrigation) increase water use efficiency (WUE) up to 80-90% compared to traditional surface/flood irrigation (30-40%). Micro-irrigation reduces weed growth, minimizes energy costs, enables fertigation, and lowers labor requirements.

2. DRIP IRRIGATION SYSTEMS
- Components: Control head (pump, sand/screen filters, pressure regulator, fertigation tank/venturi injector), main pipe (HDPE/PVC), sub-mains, and lateral lines (LLDPE) fitted with drippers/emitters.
- Design Parameters:
  * Emitter Discharge Rates: 2 LPH, 4 LPH, or 8 LPH based on soil texture (clay soils require lower LPH to prevent ponding).
  * Operating Pressure: Maintain operating pressure between 1.0 kg/cm² and 1.5 kg/cm².
  * Crop Suitability: Sugarcane, banana, cotton, citrus, papaya, tomato, brinjal, chili, polyhouse crops.
- Maintenance: Flush laterals bi-weekly. Clean gravel and screen filters regularly. Acid treatment with dilute hydrochloric acid (0.5% HCl) to dissolve carbonate emitter clogging.

3. SPRINKLER IRRIGATION SYSTEMS
- Types: Portable sprinkler systems, semi-permanent systems, and micro-sprinklers/foggers.
- Operating Parameters:
  * Nozzle Size: 3.2 mm to 4.8 mm operating at 2.5 kg/cm² to 3.5 kg/cm² pressure.
  * Overlap: Ensure 65% overlap of spray diameter under windy conditions to maintain uniform distribution coefficient (> 85%).
  * Crop Suitability: Wheat, mustard, pulses, groundnut, soybean, tea/coffee plantations.

4. SCHEME INCENTIVES & SUBSIDY STRUCTURE
- Small and Marginal Farmers: Eligible for 55% financial assistance/subsidy on benchmark cost of drip/sprinkler installation.
- Other Farmers: Eligible for 45% financial assistance/subsidy.
