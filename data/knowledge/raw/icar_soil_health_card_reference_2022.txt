1. PURPOSE
The Soil Health Card (SHC) scheme provides farmers with crop-wise nutrient
recommendations based on soil tests, promoting balanced fertilisation and improved
soil health.

2. PARAMETERS REPORTED
Each Soil Health Card reports: pH, electrical conductivity (EC), organic carbon,
available nitrogen, available phosphorus, available potassium, available sulphur and
micronutrients (zinc, iron, copper, manganese, boron). Values are rated low, medium
or high.

3. INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE (general)
- Organic carbon: below 0.50 percent is low; 0.50-0.75 percent medium; above 0.75
  percent high (values vary by soil type and region).
- Available nitrogen: low below 280 kg/ha; medium 280-560 kg/ha; high above 560 kg/ha.
- Available phosphorus: low below 10 kg/ha; medium 10-25 kg/ha; high above 25 kg/ha.
- Available potassium: low below 120 kg/ha; medium 120-280 kg/ha; high above 280 kg/ha.
These ranges are indicative and must be interpreted with local laboratory standards.

4. NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES
Apply nutrients based on soil test values and crop requirement; prefer balanced NPK with
secondary and micronutrients; combine organic sources (FYM, compost, green manure) with
inorganic fertilisers; and split nitrogen to match crop demand.

5. LIMITATIONS
Soil test values are point-in-time estimates from sampled fields and do not replace field
diagnosis. Recommendations must be adapted to crop, season, irrigation and local
conditions. This reference does not contain any actual field soil-test measurements.
